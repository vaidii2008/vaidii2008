## About me

I did my undergrad in Electronics and Communication Engineering, freelanced as a MERN developer for a year, and then came to Ireland for an MSc in AI/ML at the University of Limerick. I'm based in Dublin now.

I enjoyed the ML side of the MSc, but the part I keep coming back to is the engineering around a model: the API and database it sits behind, and whatever keeps running after the notebook is closed. Most of what's on this profile is me practising that.

## What I'm working on

### [DocQA](https://github.com/vaidii2008/docqa)
Upload documents, ask questions, get answers grounded in what's actually in them (RAG). It's Next.js 15 and TypeScript on Postgres with pgvector, Prisma and Auth.js, tested with Vitest and Playwright, with CI on GitHub Actions. It runs on Vercel and Neon. I'm now extending it into something closer to an enterprise knowledge assistant.

### [Slipstream](https://github.com/vaidii2008/slipstream-exchange)
A crypto exchange simulator with its own matching engine (price-time priority), order books, market and limit orders, and live market data over WebSockets. It keeps event replay and order-book snapshots so it can rebuild its state after a restart. I'm writing every line of it by hand.

### [own-db](https://github.com/vaidii2008/own-db)
A database written from scratch in TypeScript. I wanted to understand how databases store and find data, and building one seemed like the most honest way to find out.

### Binless
A serverless Python app that helps independent grocers order fresh stock so less of it ends up in the bin. It's also the first proper app I've built in Python.

## A few things I'm proud of

- My MSc dissertation, GE-RAG, used grammatical evolution to evolve query reformulation strategies for RAG. It was graded 4.0.
- A paper from my ECE days: *CMOS Low Noise Amplifier Design Optimization Techniques* (a 950 MHz Zigbee LNA), published by Taylor & Francis / CRC Press in 2024.
- I trained Vanilla and Double DQN agents on Atari's Atlantis for 2M steps on an A100. Vanilla DQN averaged around 5,341 reward over the last 100 episodes, against roughly 533 for a random agent. (It was supposed to be Video Pinball, but the cluster nodes kept failing.)
- I reworked a diabetes prediction model and found a data-leakage bug that had inflated the original accuracy by 11 points.
- Skin lesion classification on HAM10000 ([repo](https://github.com/vaidii2008/xception-dermoscopy-cv)) with an ImageNet-pretrained Xception and patient metadata fused in. I used 3-fold cross-validation, ablations, and Grad-CAM to check what the model was actually looking at.
- I sent a fix to [ansys/pyfluent](https://github.com/ansys/pyfluent) to get one of its ML examples working with Keras 3.
- My team built edge AI apps at the LERO x Qualcomm hackathon.
